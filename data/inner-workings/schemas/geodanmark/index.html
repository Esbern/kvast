Minimum Interpretation Rules

  • GeoDanmark is a direct-geometry reference layer, not a normalized join graph like CVR.
  • The 73 feature classes span 9 thematic chapters (Bygninger, Bebyggelse, Trafik, Teknik, Natur, Hydro, Topografi, Diverse, DHMTilpasning) and route to 15 distinct leaves.
  • Many feature classes are topographic manifestations of broader concepts — always enter through the correct leaf first.
  • Spec reference: GeoDanmark Spec 6.0.2

Access Strategy

  • Use GeoDanmark when geometry quality is primary.
  • Join to DAR, BBR, or other registers when legal or administrative semantics matter more than topographic geometry.
  • Prefer direct feature extraction for bounded areas and campaign-aware comparison for change analysis.

For the question of a leaf

Buildings

Collection method: cartographic compilation

GeoDanmark provides the authoritative building footprint polygons as part of the national topographic dataset. These are photogrammetrically measured outlines.

Spatial Access Path

GeoDanmark.bygning → polygon geometry (direct, no joins needed)

Join to BBR: Match GeoDanmark footprints to BBR buildings via shared spatial location or building identifier to combine attributes + geometry.

Temporal Model

Bitemporal in BBR. GeoDanmark footprints are updated periodically (annual campaign). Historical building states via virkningFra/virkningTil in BBR.
