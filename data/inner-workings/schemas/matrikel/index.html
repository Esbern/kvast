Matrikel is Denmark’s authoritative cadastral register for legally defined land parcels. It is a core component of Grunddatamodellen.

This page inherits shared contracts from Grunddatamodellen and only documents Matrikel-specific interpretation:

Minimum Interpretation Rules

  • jordstykke is the parcel anchor.
  • ejerlav supplies the cadastral district needed to interpret parcel identifiers.
  • Boundary and survey entities describe legal structure, not independent thematic layers.
  • Ownership does not live here; join to Ejerfortegnelsen when ownership is the question.

Access Strategy

  • Use direct parcel geometry for legal boundary analysis.
  • Join to Ejerfortegnelsen for ownership and to Ejendomsbeliggenhed for address-facing property linkage.
  • Prefer current-state filtering for operational mapping and valid-time slicing for historical cadastre work.

For the question of a leaf

Cadastral Parcels

Collection method: administrative register

Matrikel is the authoritative cadastral register.

Spatial Access Path

jordstykke → geometri (polygon — direct, no joins needed)

Cadastral parcels carry their own polygon geometry directly. The jordstykke entity IS the parcel with its boundary.

Entities

EntityRole
jordstykkeThe parcel: boundary polygon, area, matrikelnummer
ejerlavCadastral district grouping parcels
skelforretningBoundary survey event (provenance of the boundary)

Key Attributes

AttributeDescription
matrikelnummerParcel identifier within the ejerlav
ejerlavskodeCadastral district code
registreretarealRegistered area (m²)
geometriParcel boundary polygon

Temporal Model

Bitemporal. Parcel boundaries change through subdivision, merger, and resurvey — all tracked with virkningFra/virkningTil.

Property Valuation

Collection method: administrative register

Matrikel provides the parcel geometry that gives valuations their spatial extent. Join via BFE number.

Temporal Model

Valuation year (aar) defines the temporal slice. Only completed (“locked”) valuations are published. Historical valuations remain accessible. The underlying bitemporal model (registreringFra/virkningFra) tracks when each record was registered versus when the valuation became effective.
