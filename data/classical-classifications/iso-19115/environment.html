ISO 19115 Definition: Environmental resources, protection, and conservation.

SPHERE Mapping

A classic thread theme. The monitoring infrastructure (stations, sensors, regulations) belongs in Anthroposphere Services. But what is monitored spans all physical spheres — air quality (Atmosphere), water quality (Hydrosphere), soil contamination (Geosphere), and ecosystem health (Biosphere).

Primary sphere: Anthroposphere Subsphere(s): anthroposphere_services

This theme bridges multiple SPHERE domains:

  • Thread → Geosphere
