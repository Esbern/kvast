Abstract

Kortet indeholder samtlige anmeldte marker fra det seneste ansøgningsår. Ved en mark forstås et sammenhængende areal, hvorpå der dyrkes én afgrøde. Hver enkelt mark indeholder attributter med angivelse af marknummer, journalnummer, markens geometriske areal, ansøgt grundbetaling (1/0), afgrødekode, afgrødenavn og markbloknummer. Kortet anvendes ved administrationen af sager, der knytter sig til geografisk stedfæstelse af dyrknings-arealer, primært af EU’s arealbaserede støtteordninger.

Attributes

The publisher has no attribute description for Marker: the metadata names the attributes in one sentence (“marknummer, journalnummer, markens geometriske areal, ansøgt grundbetaling (1/0), afgrødekode, afgrødenavn og markbloknummer”), and the WFS schema adds only the data types. The table below is put together from that sentence, the agency’s crop code lists and crop distribution tables, and the data itself. Where a meaning is our reading of the data rather than the publisher’s statement, the table says so.

Marker is a register of applications: each feature is a field as the farmer drew it on the online field map (Internet Markkort, IMK) and reported it in the Fællesskema for basic payment that year. It shows what was declared, not what was surveyed, and the current year is preliminary and is updated as applications come in.

Crop codes

The meaning of Afgkode is set each year in the agency’s list Oversigt over afgrødekoder til Fællesskema, for example the list for 2026. For every code it gives the crop, whether it is eligible for basic payment, whether it counts as agricultural land, a crop category (number and name) and whether it is in the rotation (overview of categories).

Codes are not stable over time: crops get new codes and codes get new meanings (permanent grass is 276 in 2014 and coded differently later). To compare years, translate each year’s codes with that year’s list, or group them into crop categories. Data/Inner workings/Code lists/danish-crop-codes-afgkode has every code with an English name, its group and category, and simplified crop classes for comparing years, as a table to join to Marker.

Watch out

  • CVR, Journalnr and the older applicant numbers identify the applicant, and a sole proprietorship is a person. Use them to link fields of the same farm, not to name farmers in a report.
  • IMK_areal and GBanmeldt are the area of the whole field. If you clip fields to a study area, compute the area again.

Affiliation history

Datasets change hands when ministries are reorganised, and reports, maps and older metadata keep the name of the agency of their time. Every agency this dataset has been filed under in SemanticGIS is kept here; the dates are when SemanticGIS recorded it, not when the responsibility moved.

  • Styrelsen for Grøn Arealomlægning og Vandmiljø (current): recorded 2026-11-11, in Danmarks Miljøportal (dataset owner). The agency was created in September 2024 under the new Ministeriet for Grøn Trepart (the green tripartite agreement); most of Landbrugsstyrelsen’s staff and parts of Miljøstyrelsen moved to it (Altinget, 25 September 2024).
  • Landbrugsstyrelsen: recorded 2026-04-18 to 2026-11-11, in the geodata-info.dk metadata.
