A register: data that exists because of a standing public task. Why it exists, its history and its law are not written yet (they need a source). What the data looks like is described by the schema GeoDanmark.

As described in the source catalogue

Danmarks Geografi - GeoDanmark grunddata (sgis-000936)

GeoDanmark omfatter informationer om bl.a. bygninger, veje, vandløb, søer, skove og tilpasningslag (DHMtilpasning). Data giver et landsdækkede fællesoffentligt geografisk administrationsgrundlag som er egnet til sagsbehandling, planlægning, sammenstilling med andre data og visuelle præsentationer af Danmarks geografi.

Quality

From the GeoDanmark specification 6.0.2, chapter 2.0 Krav, regler og kvalitet (2.1.5 Nøjagtighed i forhold til GSD; 2.6.7 Fuldstændighed). It holds for every dataset of this register.

WherePhoto pixelAccuracy, planAccuracy, heightUseful from
towns (inside the area polygon: all object types)0.1–0.15 m0.1–0.15 m0.15–0.2 m1:1,000
the countryside (outside it: fewer object types)0.4 m0.4 m0.75 m1:2,000

Useful from is the largest scale at which the error stays below about 0.2 mm on the map; zoom in further and the errors show. The rule is ours, not the specification’s.

Completeness: errors allowed against the orthophoto: buildings 1 %, road centrelines with a road code 1 %, road centrelines without 3 %, other points 5 %, other lines 3 %, other areas 3 %.

Caution

The accuracy holds for well-defined points (manhole covers, drains). Trees, hedges and unpaved roads are much less accurate; coasts, lakes and land-use borders are as on the day of the photograph. Surveyed features are about twice as accurate.
