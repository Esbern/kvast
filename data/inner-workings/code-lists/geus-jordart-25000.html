GEUS deposit symbols, Digital Surface Geology Map 1:25 000 (version 7)

Mnemonic letter codes (deposit type, age, lithology), Gravesen & Fredericia (1984). Stacked codes = two deposits within 1 m; hyphenated = a mixture.
