Abstract

Maps of soil organic carbon (SOC, % by mass) for Denmark in 10 m resolution, for the depth intervals 0–30, 30–60, 60–100 and 100–200 cm. Made at the Department of Agroecology, Aarhus University, in 2024, partly with DHI, SEGES and the University of Copenhagen in the research project DIGIJORD, by machine learning from soil samples, Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series and environmental layers (Møller et al. 2025). Version 1.1, 4 July 2024. Each depth has the predicted SOC and its uncertainty (standard deviation, 5 % and 95 % quantiles). The README asks users to cite Beucher et al. (2023, 2024) and Møller et al. (2024a, 2024b).

One published dataset, two named maps

The report behind the data, Møller et al. (2024a), does not speak of “Kulstofkort”. It names map collections, and the published layers come from two of them:

  • Tekstur2024 — texture, SOC and carbonate at four depths, mean / sd / q05 / q95 from 100 bootstrap runs. All SOC layers below 30 cm and the topsoil uncertainty layers come from here.
  • Kulstof2022 — the topsoil (0–30 cm) SOC of Tekstur2024 combined with Tørv2022. The topsoil mean, the classes and the combination layer come from here.

Each layer in the version therefore carries a product field. This is a pattern the model has to handle in general: what a publisher distributes as one dataset is not always what the scientists name and cite.

Everything that differs between releases — date, method, inputs, layers, extent, files — is on the version.

Versions: kulstofkort-10-m-v1-1.
